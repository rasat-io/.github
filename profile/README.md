Rasat builds automation for small and medium-sized businesses: code where precision matters, AI where scale matters, and people where judgment matters. *Rasat* is Turkish for observation, and every project starts by watching how the work really gets done.

### What we build

| | |
|---|---|
| [3D Furniture Configurator](https://www.rasat.io/configurator/) | Customers design a built-to-measure piece in 3D and send it to the maker as an order request. The demo runs on sample data. |
| AI Content Production | On-brand images, video and copy, produced with generative AI and reviewed by people before anything ships. |
| AI-Native Transformation | Workflow automation, and making a business findable and usable by AI assistants such as ChatGPT and Claude. |
